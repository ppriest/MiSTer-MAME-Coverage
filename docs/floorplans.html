MiSTer core floorplans

Where each core's logic sits on the DE10-nano's Cyclone V (5CSEBA6), read from the latest compiled build of each revision with scripts/floorplan.py; nothing was recompiled for this page. One cell per LAB, M10K or DSP site, coloured by the block owning most of it, brighter when fuller. The thin columns are M10K and DSP; the empty area at the top right is the HPS; grey is the MiSTer framework. Colours mean the same kind of block in every core (red main CPU, yellow/orange sound, green tilemaps, cyan sprites, purple work RAM, blue SDRAM). LUT counts are combinational cells, two per ALM. Konami GX's T34 revision is missing: its build has no loadable database.

MiSTer-kun sits on the HPS: the ARM side, where the fabric has no logic sites. Mascot by HeWhoisRed, remastered by baxysquare (github.com/baxysquare/mister_kun).
